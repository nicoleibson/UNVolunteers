⑥ DEPLOYMENT & ECOSYSTEM STRATEGY

Modular Project Mainline Update

Packaged as a Play System Update under com.google.android.connectivity.apex.

  • Coverage: Android 10+ — 85%+ of global active devices
  • Deployment: Silent OTA, no user interaction required
  • Permissions: Signature-level MANAGE_EMERGENCY_BROADCAST

Google Play Services Companion APK

Legacy fallback for Android 6.0–9.0 (API 23–28).

  • Role: Passive Scout relay — scan-only, no active beaconing
  • Function: Caches encrypted BLE payloads, forwards on network recovery

Android Compatibility Definitions

Legal and architectural rules for next-generation devices.

  • CDD §7.4.3: BLE Long Range and UWB hardware must pass emergency beacon CTS
  • CTS bound: Beacon launch within 500 ms of Cell Broadcast interrupt
